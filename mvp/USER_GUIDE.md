# Team Sentiment Pulse - User Guide

## Overview Page
- Open `/` to see KPI cards:
  - Total teams
  - Total members
  - Active at-risk members
  - Average sentiment score
  - Current cycle response rate
- Team cards show risk badge and trend arrow.

## Team Detail
- Open `/team/[id]` from any team card.
- Review:
  - Team trend (latest 6 cycles)
  - Member composite score and delta
  - Latest comments per member

## Member Profile
- Open `/member/[id]` from team detail.
- Review:
  - Last 6-cycle timeline
  - Disengagement score
  - Active alerts
  - Assigned recommendations
  - Manager notes

## Surveys
- Open `/surveys` for pulse submissions table.
- Supports named and anonymous rows.
- Q7 bucket shown as low/moderate/high.
- Team net recommendation score shown per cycle.
- Export data via `Export CSV` button.

## Alerts
- Open `/alerts` to monitor unresolved threshold breaches.
- Prioritize critical/high first.

## Recommendations
- Open `/recommendations` for generated actions.
- Items include priority and target date.

## Retention
- Open `/retention` to sort members by disengagement score.
- Use churn badge and recommendation to prioritize interventions.

## Settings
- Open `/settings` for read-only survey configuration per team.

## Suggested Operating Cadence
- Review Alerts and Retention weekly.
- Review Team pages at end of each pulse cycle.
- Close recommendations only after action is completed.
